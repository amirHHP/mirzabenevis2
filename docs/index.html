Capabilities

Designed for Seamless Recording

Auto Captions Activation

Automatically turns on Google Meet captions as soon as you join, ensuring no part of the conversation is ever missed.

Smart Speaker Tracking

Organizes transcripts neatly by mapping exactly who spoke what, matching avatars, names, and precise seconds.

Instant Export & Copy

Download formatted transcript logs to plain text files (.txt) or copy them to your clipboard with a single click.

Session History Archive

Access, rename, review, or copy the archives of all your past captured Google Meet sessions from the extension popup interface.

100% Privacy Focused

All conversations are captured and saved locally on your device. Absolutely no data is sent to external servers or cloud repositories.

Lightweight Footprint

Extremely lightweight build with zero overhead. It runs silently in the background without affecting browser or call performance.

Setup Guide

Get MirzaBenevis in Minutes

  • 1

    Visit Web Store

    Go to the Chrome Web Store and search for "MirzaBenevis" (Store Link coming soon).

  • 2

    Add to Chrome

    Click on the "Add to Chrome" button on the extension page.

  • 3

    Confirm Permissions

    Approve the dialog box permissions. The browser will download and install the extension automatically.

  • 4

    Ready to Record

    Join any Google Meet. MirzaBenevis will auto-start capturing logs when captions are displayed.

  • 1

    Download source ZIP

    Click the download button on this page or clone the codebase directly from our GitHub repository.

  • 2

    Extract ZIP

    Extract the downloaded file contents onto a folder on your computer.

  • 3

    Open Extension Dashboard

    Open a tab in Google Chrome and type chrome://extensions/ in the URL address bar.

  • 4

    Enable Developer Mode

    Toggle the "Developer Mode" switch in the top-right corner to enabled.

  • 5

    Load Unpacked

    Click the "Load Unpacked" button in the top-left, and select the folder directory where you extracted the extension.
